FarVPN Subscription Guide

Quick Start · Multi-Platform · Open Source Clients

📖 What is a Subscription Link?
A subscription link is FarVPN's unique configuration URL for your account. It contains all your node information and encryption parameters. Simply import the subscription link into a supported client, and it will automatically fetch all available nodes — no need to configure each server manually.
How it works: Copy your subscription link from FarVPN (Mine → VIP Center → Copy subscription link) → Open your client → Import the subscription → Choose a node → Connect with one click.
🔑 Get Your Subscription Link
Follow these steps to get your personal subscription link:
  • 1
    Open the FarVPN App → Go to "Mine"
  • 2
    Tap "VIP Center"
  • 3
    Tap "Copy subscription link" — the link will be copied to your clipboard
  • 4
    Paste the link into your preferred client to import your nodes
💡 Tip: If you can't find the subscription link in the app, please contact our support team for help.
Keep your subscription link private — do not share it with others.
📥 Import Methods
Most clients support these common import methods. Choose whichever works best for you:
  • 1
    One-click import (Recommended): Open the subscription link in your browser, and it will automatically redirect to the installed client to complete the import.
  • 2
    Copy & Paste: Copy the subscription link → Open your client → Find "Import Subscription" or "Add Configuration" → Paste the link
  • 3
    QR Code: Tap "Show QR Code" in the FarVPN App, then scan it with your client

Windows · macOS · Linux
A modern cross-platform GUI client based on the Clash Meta kernel. Supports subscription links, rule-based routing, proxy groups, and more. One of the most popular clients available.
⬇ Download GitHub

① Download and install Clash Verge
② Open the app → Go to "Subscriptions" → Click "Import"

❓ FAQ
Q: My subscription link has expired. What should I do?
Copy a fresh subscription link from the FarVPN App and update your subscription in the client.
Q: I can't connect to any nodes?
Try switching to a different node, or check your network connection. If all nodes fail to connect, please contact support.
Q: The client shows a "Parse Error"?
Make sure you copied the full subscription link correctly. Some clients may require you to select the correct subscription type.
Q: The connection is slow?
Try switching to a less congested node, or use "Auto Select" mode to let the client choose the best node for you.
Q: Can I use the subscription on multiple devices?
Yes, your subscription can be used on multiple devices. However, the number of simultaneous connections depends on your plan.
